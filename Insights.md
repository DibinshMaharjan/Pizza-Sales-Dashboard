Pizza Sales Dashboard Insights

Based on the analysis performed in Power BI, the dashboard provides the following key insights:

### 1. Busiest Day

**Friday** has the highest number of orders among the days of the week, making it the busiest day.

### 2. Order Time

Order activity is highest around **midday**, particularly around **12 PM and 1 PM**. Another strong period occurs around **5 PM–6 PM**.

### 3. Time of Day

**Afternoon** accounts for the largest share of orders, followed by **Evening**.

### 4. Top Pizza by Revenue

**The Thai Chicken Pizza** generates the highest sales revenue among the pizzas shown in the dashboard.

### 5. Most Ordered Pizzas

**The Classic Deluxe Pizza, The Hawaiian Pizza, and The Pepperoni Pizza** are among the pizzas with the highest order quantities.

### 6. Best-Performing Category

The **Classic** pizza category generates the highest revenue among the four categories.

### 7. Overall Sales Performance

The dashboard records approximately:

- **Total Revenue:** $817.86K
- **Total Orders:** 21.35K
- **Total Pizzas Sold:** 49.57K

## 📌 Business Takeaway

The analysis indicates that customer demand is particularly strong during **afternoon and early evening periods**, with **Friday** being the busiest day. Classic-category pizzas and specific high-performing pizzas contribute significantly to overall sales.

These insights can help a pizza business optimize staffing, inventory planning, promotions, and product-focused marketing around high-demand periods and products.
